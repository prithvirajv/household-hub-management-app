# Phone check list (after a deploy)

I can measure the layout in a simulated phone and a copy of the real household, but not on a real iPhone. After a deploy, these take about five minutes. Hard-refresh first (or close and reopen the installed app).

1. **Home.** Does the first screen show content within a thumb's reach of the top (title and Sign out on one line; month and four icon buttons on the next; the figures as a small 2 x 2 grid)? Is "Bills & goals" short, with "N more" folded below?
2. **Transactions.** Each row is two short lines (payee and amount; date and subcategory) with a ⋯ on the right. Tap ⋯ on one row: account, split, receipt, delete and tags open under it. Nothing runs off the right edge. Pick an account in the filter: the list still fits.
3. **Transactions, a Zelle deposit.** Tap **Pays back a charge?**: your card purchases come first, with the account and a reason on each choice, and the text is readable.
4. **Budget.** Subcategory rows stack: name and buttons; Planned, Spent, Remaining; Due date and Owner. The Income rows stack too. No sideways scrolling. "Left to budget" appears once.
5. **Paychecks.** Tap the × on a subcategory chip: it should be easy to hit, and only that chip goes.
6. **Wealth.** "Closed accounts" is folded under the live ones; tap it and your closed accounts appear with their "Closed <date>" tag.
7. **Meals.** Recipes > a recipe > **Use in Meals**: a green "Planning <recipe>" bar. Tap any open slot: the recipe is already chosen; press **Plan**. In a slot's picker, recipe names are dark and readable, and the Search box narrows a long list.
8. **Top of the screen.** Scroll any page: the clock and battery stay readable (no content showing through them).
9. **Dark mode** (🌙 in the header): pills, buttons and green amounts are readable on every page above.
10. **Sync.** Wealth > Linked accounts: **Sync now**, the ▾ and the **From** date on one row; the result list under it after a sync.

If anything differs, note the screen and what you see (a screenshot is ideal) and I will fix it.
