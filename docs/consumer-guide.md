# Famelo Consumer Guide

Famelo brings household budgets, transactions, calendars, chores, meals, goals, and shared planning into one workspace.

## Getting Started

1. Create an account and choose the household country.
2. Add income in **Budget**.
3. Create budget categories and subcategories, then assign planned amounts.
4. Add transactions and assign each one to a budget line.
5. Add calendar events, recurring chores, birthdays, meals, and goals as needed.

## Multiple Households

Use **Current household** in the sidebar to switch between homes or countries. Each household stores its own currency, budget, transactions, calendar, meals, goals, debts, and assets.

## Invitations

Household owners can choose which areas to share and send an invitation by email.

- Use the exact email address that received the invitation.
- Open the direct acceptance link or select **Accept invitation** on the sign-in screen.
- Enter the one-time invite code.
- New users create a password of at least 12 characters.
- Existing users enter their current password.

Invitation codes become invalid after acceptance.

## Password Recovery

Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. Famelo sends a one-time reset link that expires after 30 minutes. Check Spam and All Mail if the message does not appear in the inbox.

## Budget and Transactions

Create categories and subcategories in **Budget**. Planned values, category totals, available money, and transaction spending update together. Transactions keep their historical category labels even if a category is later removed.

## Calendar and Chores

Calendar supports events, reminders, annual birthdays, and recurring chores. Weekly chores generate future occurrences automatically.

## Meals and Recipes

Save reusable recipes under **Recipes**, then choose them from the weekly **Meals** planner. Ingredients can be used to build the grocery list.

## Goals and Wealth

Use **Goals** for sinking funds. Use **Wealth** for debts, payments, assets, liabilities, and net worth.

## Reports and Export

Reports summarize category spending and budget health. The header download control exports the selected month as CSV.
